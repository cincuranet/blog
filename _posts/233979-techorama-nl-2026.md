Next week [Techorama in Utrecht][1] is going to happen. The _medieval edition_ in Belgium was fun and I'm looking forward to it again in Netherlands. I was lucky and [2 of my sessions][2] were selected.

<!-- excerpt -->

First one is _Azure Spot instances as your secret weapon_.

> Almost everyone knows virtual machines in Azure. But with a little effort, it is possible to modify the application and use Spot instances, which can be an interesting financial alternative. In this session we will look at what Spot instances are, how they differ from common VMs and how to use them effectively.

And other one is _Weird C# features you (don't) need to know about_.

> C# is a mature language with a surface most developers know well - but beneath it lurk features that are obscure, misunderstood, or simply bizarre. This talk digs into the dark corners.

Hope to meet you there.
